📜 Field Notes

Village: Safe place to recover and cook. Stand near the campfire and press Interact.

Dungeon: Slimes and skeletons roam the halls. Defeat them for meat, mushrooms, herbs, and stone.

Combat: Tap Attack when a monster is close. Hold Block before its attack lands to prevent damage.

Swamplands: Find the ancient stone to reveal the dungeon. Clear each level to open its chest.

Spells: Equip four at home, then cast them from the spellbook. Magic regenerates over time.

Hearthward Charm: Takes 15 seconds and all your magic to teleport home.

Hunger: Falls slowly while in the dungeon. At low hunger, you lose health. Cook food back in the village.
