📜 Field Notes

Village: Safe place to recover and cook at the fire.

Swamplands: Find the ancient stone and gather moss.

Whispering Woods: Help the ranger hunt the brutes.

Frostfell Highlands: Find the oracle and hunt the frost wyrms.

Dungeon: Explore the stone halls; the western arch returns you to the swamplands.

Controls: Move with the joystick, WASD, or arrow keys. Space interacts, O holds block, P attacks, I opens your pack, F toggles fullscreen, and Escape closes panels.

Quest: Gather 3 dungeon stones and 2 herbs, then return to the village elder.
