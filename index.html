📜 Field Notes

Village: Safe place to recover and cook at the fire.

Swamplands: Find the ancient stone and gather moss.

Dungeon: Explore the stone halls, defeat monsters, and return through the western arch to the swamplands.

Controls: Move with the joystick, WASD, or arrow keys. Hold O to block, press P to attack, and press I to open your pack.

Quest: Gather 3 dungeon stones and 2 herbs, then return to the village elder.
